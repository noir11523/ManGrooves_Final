## Final production prompt set

All assets used the built-in image generation mode. These normalized prompts are the regeneration record for the selected outputs.

### Landing hero

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: responsive environmental-monitoring landing-page hero
Primary request: Create a wide documentary photograph of a healthy tropical mangrove restoration site in coastal Cebu, Philippines, with mature trees, visible prop roots, calm tidal water, and young planted mangroves.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape; mangroves concentrated on the right; generous uncluttered sky and water on the left for readable page copy; natural eye-level perspective.
Lighting/mood: warm sunrise, hopeful and credible, realistic field-documentary light.
Constraints: botanically plausible mangrove habitat; realistic water and roots; no people, buildings, boats, text, labels, logos, or watermark.
```

### Shared field-guide direction

```text
Use case: scientific-educational
Asset type: mobile mangrove health checklist comparison guide
Primary request: Create one photorealistic comparison image whose equal-width panels show the requested observable conditions clearly on mangroves in plausible coastal habitat.
Style/medium: close documentary field photography, consistent subject scale, camera angle, neutral daylight, and natural color across panels.
Composition/framing: clean panel separation only; the subject fills each panel and remains easy to compare on a phone.
Constraints: scientifically plausible, visually unambiguous, no captions, letters, numbers, icons, decorative borders, people, logos, or watermark.
```

Apply that shared direction with these panel sequences:

- `leaf-color.png`: three panels—healthy deep-green leaves; light/yellow-green leaves; brown/yellow-brown declining leaves.
- `leaf-condition.png`: four panels—smooth healthy leaves; viable naturally waxy/curled/upward leaves; leaves with holes/dark spots; brittle dry leaves.
- `pests.png`: three panels—no visible pest damage; a few holes or bites; severe holes and visible webbing/infestation.
- `roots.png`: four panels—firm intact mangrove roots; loose roots with localized damage; roots exposed by obvious erosion; dead or rotten roots.
- `bark.png`: four panels—intact smooth bark; slight peeling/small cracks; naturally rough fibrous or fissured bark; severe missing bark/large damage.
- `bio-indicators.png`: five panels—mangrove crab; heron/egret; juvenile fish in shallow water; mangrove snails; butterfly, each visibly associated with plausible habitat.
- `negative-signs.png`: three panels—a dense mosquito presence; plastic litter caught among mangrove roots; an empty degraded mudflat with no visible animal life.

All generation prompts required scientifically plausible documentary imagery, consistent neutral daylight, clear comparison panels, and no text, labels, logos, watermarks, or decorative borders. The visual guides support observation; final ecological interpretation remains with a CCENRO expert.
